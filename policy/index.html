Privacy Policy

Antify Share by Antify Studio. Last updated: November 1, 2026.

Antify Share is a tool for sending files directly from one device to another. It has no accounts, no servers that store your data, and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Antify Studio does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, files or usage data.

How your files move

Files go straight from the sender’s device to the receiver’s device over an encrypted peer-to-peer (WebRTC) connection. They are never uploaded to a server owned by us or anyone else. Received files are held in your browser’s memory until you close or reload the page, so download what you want to keep.

Photos

If “Shrink photos” is on, images are resized and re-encoded on your own device before sending. This also removes metadata such as location and camera details. Videos, PDFs and text files are sent unchanged.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan the QR code on the other device. Video is processed on your device, is not recorded, and is never sent anywhere.

Third-party services

  • A public STUN server (stun.l.google.com) is contacted to help two devices find each other. It sees your IP address, but not your files.
  • The QR code libraries (QRCode.js and jsQR) are loaded from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Cloudflare may see your IP address when the page loads.

These services have their own privacy policies.

Cookies and storage

Antify Share does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or local storage.

Children

Antify Share does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
